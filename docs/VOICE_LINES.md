# rewire — voice lines

Four speakers, four audio files. Each speaker reads their own section top to bottom, in this order. The lines are then cut apart and placed in the video.

## How to record

- One file per speaker: `speaker1`, `speaker2`, `speaker3`, `speaker4`. Any phone voice memo format.
- Put them in `HighWire-work/video/voice/`.
- **Stay silent for two full seconds between lines.** That silence is how the lines are cut apart.
- Inside a line, keep going. No pause longer than a breath.
- Do not say the line numbers.
- Fluffed a line? Stay silent two seconds, say it again, and say which line you repeated when you send the file.
- Quiet room, phone a hand's width from your mouth, a brisk pace.
